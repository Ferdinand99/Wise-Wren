# Wise Wren – glossary (Russian, `ru`)

Draft: not yet reviewed by native speakers.

Wren's own strings are written from English with these words. Core strings shared with Vigilant Vole come from
vole-kit and follow [Vigilant Vole's glossary](https://github.com/Ferdinand99/Vigilant-Vole/blob/main/locales/glossary/ru.md).

## Style
- Neutral register, imperative or impersonal ("Введите…", "Не удалось…"); no "ты", "вы" only when unavoidable.
- Quotation marks «…» (inner „…“). Plain «е» (no «ё»). Sentence case. Ellipsis `…` kept as in English.
- Plurals: one / few / many / other (other = genitive singular, e.g. `{n} сервера`).
- Kept in English: Docker, Compose, Unraid, `agent.toml`, `WW_SETUP_TOKEN`, `WW_PUBLIC_URL`, `docker run`, JSON, webhook, and other product, protocol and
  provider names.
