# Wise Wren – glossary (Korean, `ko`)

Draft: not yet reviewed by native speakers.

Wren's own strings are written from English with these words. Core strings shared with Vigilant Vole come from
vole-kit and follow [Vigilant Vole's glossary](https://github.com/Ferdinand99/Vigilant-Vole/blob/main/locales/glossary/ko.md).

## Style
- 해요체 for sentences (Apple style), noun-ending labels for buttons, headings and tabs. No exclamation marks.
- Quotes: “ ” (and ‘ ’). Particles are never attached to `{placeholders}`; sentences are rephrased
  (e.g. `“{name}” 서버를 삭제했어요`, `{name}: …`).
- Counts: no plural forms; `서버 {n}개`. Standard loanword spellings.
- Kept in Latin script: Docker, Compose, Unraid, `agent.toml`, `WW_SETUP_TOKEN`, `WW_PUBLIC_URL`, `docker run`, JSON, and other product and protocol names.
